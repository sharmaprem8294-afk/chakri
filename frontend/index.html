Guide

How it works-for workers

  1. Tap the mic and speak-your profession, years of experience, where you work, and what you're strong at.
  2. Watch the transcript fill in live, so you know it heard you right.
  3. Review your profile once Chakri structures it into role, experience, and location.
  4. Get discovered-recruiters search by exactly those fields, and a matching profile surfaces automatically.

Guide

How it works-for recruiters

  1. Switch to "Hiring" using the toggle on the homepage.
  2. Just say abt role, experience, or location- no resume to sift through.
  3. Browse matched profiles, each built from a real spoken description.
  4. Reach out directly to the workers who fit what you need.

Company

About Chakri

Chakri means "job" and we built this for people who are better with their hands than with a keyboard: electricians,welders, machinists, CNC operators, technicians. Most hiring platforms assume you're comfortable typing out a resume. We don't.

Say what you do, out loud, and Chakri turns it into a profile recruiters can actually search by role, by years of experience, by where you work. No forms. No formatting. Just the work, in your own words.
